A bilingual information-design system that turns the financial-planning process into a clear five-meeting roadmap and supports clients with structured planning documents.

02 / SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS

Turning discovery into structured information.

The supporting financial-planning forms organize monthly income and expenses, assets and liabilities, insurance coverage, estate and legal documents, and a checklist of records clients may need to provide. Together, the roadmap and forms help establish what information is needed at each stage of the planning conversation.

04 / MY ROLE

Designing for clarity and participation.

I helped translate the advisory process into client-facing materials that clarify both what the advisor presents and what the client contributes. The Spanish version was adapted for bilingual communication, with terminology and tone reviewed for readability rather than treated as a literal translation.
