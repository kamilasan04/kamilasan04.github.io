ONEFLORIDA FINANCIAL GROUP · INTERNSHIP PROJECT

Client Outreach &
Relationship Tracker

A practical Excel system I developed during my financial-planning internship to centralize prospect information, prioritize outreach, and make relationship management easier.

EXCELWORKFLOW DESIGNPROCESS IMPROVEMENTAI-ASSISTED DEVELOPMENT
01 / THE PROBLEM

Turning scattered outreach into a repeatable process.

As I began building relationships with potential clients, I wanted one place to organize who I had contacted, how I knew them, relevant relationship context, and how confident I felt about future outreach. I created a lightweight system that made that information easier to review and act on.

02 / INTERACTIVE DEMO

See how the tracker works.

This public demo uses fictional sample records. No real client or prospect information is displayed.

03 / SYSTEM DESIGN

Simple inputs, automatic summary.

Relationship context
Fields capture name, relationship, age, occupation, spouse, children, contact details, and notes.

1–5 confidence score
A simple rating gives each relationship an outreach-priority signal that is quick to scan.

Automatic dashboard
Excel formulas calculate total contacts, high-, medium-, and low-confidence prospects, and average confidence.

AI-assisted development
I used Claude as a development assistant while personally directing the workbook structure, workflow, fields, and intended use.

04 / IMPACT

A small tool that reduced friction in day-to-day outreach.

The project demonstrates how I approach process improvement: identify a repetitive workflow, define the information needed to make decisions, and build a straightforward system that makes the next action clearer.

Business process improvement through Excel.

Microsoft Excel · Workflow design · CRM-style organization · Process improvement · Formula-based summaries · Relationship management · AI-assisted development · Information architecture
