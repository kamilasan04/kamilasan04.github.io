01 / BUSINESS QUESTION

Where could energy conditions best support a new data center?

The project, co-designed with Intel’s Sustainability Team, asked which U.S. regions produce an energy surplus, how supply and demand change throughout the day, and which regions rely more heavily on renewable sources. The goal was to turn those patterns into a location recommendation.

02 / ANALYTICAL WORK
Net Production

Compared regional electricity generation with demand to identify markets producing an energy surplus.

Renewable Energy

Evaluated renewable-energy utilization across regions and the role of individual generation sources.

24-Hour Supply & Demand

Examined hourly patterns to understand how generation and demand changed over a full day.

Generation Stability

Used hourly differences in generation to compare consistency and volatility across markets.

03 / KEY FINDING

The Northwest emerged as a leading region.

My analysis identified the Northwest as a strong candidate because of its positive net energy production, high renewable-energy share, substantial generation capacity, and strong reliance on hydropower. The recommendation combined energy availability with sustainability considerations rather than relying on a single metric.
