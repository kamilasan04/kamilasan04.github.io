01 / PROJECT CONTEXT

What shapes the urgent care wait?

In KPMG U.S.’s Career Catalyst: Advisory Job Simulation on Forage, I took on the role of a consultant to Healthy Co. The facility wanted to improve patient satisfaction, so I explored both the length of the wait and what patients experienced while waiting.

02 / MY ANALYSIS

Comparing visits and weighing ideas

Wait times by patient group

I built Excel pivot tables and charts to compare wait times by provider, arrival time, and diagnosis group.

Download my wait-time analysis (Excel)
Impact and ease of implementation

I rated ideas on both criteria. A patient passport could help people track their progress through a visit, while separate check-in and payment desks could keep arriving and departing patients from waiting in the same line.

Download my initiative brainstorm (Word)
03 / CLIENT PRESENTATION

Turning findings into next steps

I organized the presentation around three questions for Healthy Co’s leaders. Where did the data show differences in wait times? Which practices had helped other facilities? What could Healthy Co try next?

04 / WHAT I LEARNED

Advising in an unfamiliar industry

I enjoyed working through a healthcare problem and seeing how a KPMG advisor might approach it. Storyboarding helped me decide which details from the workbook belonged in a conversation with clinic leadership.

Tools and concepts applied
