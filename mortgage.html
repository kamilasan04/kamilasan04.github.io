Mortgage Lending
Analysis

An interactive Excel case study using 12,550 Home Mortgage Disclosure Act records to examine approval outcomes across demographic groups and income categories.

EXCEL12,550 RECORDSPIVOTTABLESXLOOKUP + IFSA/B TESTING
01 / RESEARCH QUESTION

Do approval rates differ across demographic groups?

I first compared approval outcomes across racial groups, then compared Black and White applicants within lower-, middle-, and upper-income categories. The analysis documents patterns in this dataset; it does not by itself establish why those differences occurred.

02 / INTERACTIVE ANALYSIS

Explore the results.

03 / METHOD

From raw applications to comparable groups.

1 · Prepare
Used XLOOKUP to classify outcomes as approved or denied.

2 · Explore
Used PivotTables and charts to compare approval rates across racial groups.

3 · Segment
Used IFS to classify applicants as lower, middle, or upper income and compared groups within each category.

4 · Test
Used the project’s A/B testing framework for the middle-income comparison.

04 / INTERPRETATION

The disparity persisted within each income category.

In the completed analysis, Black applicants had lower approval rates than White applicants in the lower-, middle-, and upper-income groups. The middle-income test subset also showed a statistically significant difference. Additional variables and a stronger causal design would be needed to determine the cause of that disparity.

Excel analysis with statistical reasoning.
