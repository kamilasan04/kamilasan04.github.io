01 / PROJECT CONTEXT

Applying environmental economics to consulting decisions.

I completed BCG’s Climate & Sustainability job simulation on Forage while exploring sustainability consulting. The experience gave me a chance to apply concepts from my Environmental Economics course to practical business questions, including how companies measure emissions and evaluate opportunities to reduce them.

02 / MY ANALYSIS

From emissions data to potential strategies.

Emissions baselines & benchmarking

Used Excel to calculate emissions baselines and compare company data with the CDP database, examining differences in carbon emissions across companies.

Scope 1, 2 & 3 emissions

Examined the different scopes of emissions and their sources to better understand a company’s emissions profile and where reduction efforts could be directed.

Abatement-curve analysis

Analyzed abatement curves to consider the costs and emissions-reduction potential of different options, connecting economic theory with sustainability decisions.

Automotive decarbonization research

Researched articles and developed strategies for reducing Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions within the automotive industry.

03 / INVESTOR PERSPECTIVE

Considering risks alongside emissions reductions.

I analyzed the risks and concerns associated with different strategies for different investors. This helped me consider how a recommendation needs to account for financial and practical tradeoffs alongside its potential to reduce emissions.

04 / WHAT I LEARNED

Connecting data, research, and judgment.

The simulation strengthened my ability to work with unfamiliar sustainability data and connect quantitative analysis with research. My favorite part was moving beyond the theory: researching potential solutions, comparing company emissions, and thinking through the concerns that influence investment decisions.

05 / SKILLS

Tools and concepts applied.

Excel · Emissions baseline calculations · CDP benchmarking · Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions · Abatement-curve analysis · Sustainability research · Automotive decarbonization · Investor risk assessment
