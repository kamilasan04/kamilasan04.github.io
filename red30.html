01 / BUSINESS QUESTION

What does the data reveal about Red30 Tech’s performance?

I worked across financial statements, regional sales data, labor expenses, and marketing expenses to organize and analyze business performance. The workbook combines structured tables, formulas, calculated fields, and transformed datasets.

03 / WORKBOOK DESIGN

Multiple business datasets combined into one analysis.

Financial statements
Reviewed revenue, expenses, profitability, assets, liabilities, equity, and cash flow from 2014–2018.

Regional sales
Analyzed 4,976 transactions by sales region, product category, customer type, quantity, discount, and order total.

Expense data
Worked with separate labor and marketing expense datasets for 2017 and 2018.

Excel techniques
Used structured tables, formulas, calculated fields, data mapping, and transformed intermediary datasets.

04 / KEY INSIGHTS

Growth was visible in both the financial statements and transaction data.

Gross revenue increased from about $914.8M in 2014 to $1.013B in 2018, while net income rose from about $38.3M to $48.6M. In the 2017–2018 transaction dataset, Central East generated the highest sales total, and Robots generated the most revenue among product categories.
